## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `install.sh`: a one-line install that opens without the unidentified-developer prompt.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

First public release.

### Added

- A flat rug that lies over your desktop icons, with a soft drop shadow.
- Drag an edge to peel the rug back and peek underneath; it springs flat when you let go.
- Corner dots to rotate, a Design pill to switch rugs, and hold ⌥ to see through.
- Eight real antique rugs to choose from, photographed by The Met (public domain), or weave in your own image.
- Sweep every desktop icon, a Finder selection, or icons dropped on the rug into one heap underneath it. Only icon
  positions change, and *Put the Icons Back* restores each one.
- Pinch to resize, Bigger / Smaller, and Small to Cover the Desktop presets.
- `--snapshot` renders a frame offline for testing.
